## Review findings

No additional design-blocking contradictions were visible in the inspected final frames. The core
task distinctions are represented: separate capture groups and ordered endpoints; independent
filters and temporary ordinals; distinct source clocks and derived transcript limits; a concrete
160-operation inventory with three branch exits, an ancestry projection and a labeled
silver/pearl/seamed/gold legend; exact pinned recovery origin/destination; and resumable offline
state with cancellation separated from repair. These are coherent scripted representations, not
proof of product behavior.

## Cross-check boundary

Each journey includes captured native keyboard, synchronized-list, accessibility-tree,
narrow/enlarged, reduced-motion, pointer, lifecycle and fixture-effect evidence. These checks
describe this HTML prototype only. `assistiveTechnologyTested` is false; pointer coverage is DOM
action delivery rather than Xuzz gesture picking; lifecycle, source immutability, storage, branch
receipts, network availability and authoring effects are scripted fixtures. Contrast/optics and
responsive layout checks do not establish implementation rendering. “Concept supported” means the
visible state and affordance coherently represent the requested task.
