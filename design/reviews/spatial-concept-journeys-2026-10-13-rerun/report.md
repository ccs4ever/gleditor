## What is now concrete

The packs specify immutable remote edition acquisition, typed cross-media search, declared matching
and unit rules, stale-response ownership, pinned excluded selections, independent comparison states,
quiet and expanded investigations, package choices, Link cursors, reader branches, typed collections
and dense inventories. Authoring and recovery expose exact ranges, pinned bases, author identities,
private staging, explicit validation gates and same-token outcome reconciliation. Media cues retain
separate clocks and source locators. Loom state separates actual ancestry, view correspondence,
filtered order, captured page footprints and shared source identity. Offline restoration retains
exact descriptors and leaves refresh choices explicit.

The review iterations corrected missing retry and carrier choices, reader visit counters, visible
list counts, changing offline reference identities, stale request/filter labels and reading cards
that contradicted the current visit. Resolved findings remain in each runner’s report. The original
static review and raster mockups remain the frozen comparison baseline.

## Checks and their limits

Headless Chromium delivered every named transition through native DOM clicks. Each journey also has
a native Tab/Enter trace, list view, accessibility-tree snapshot, narrow/enlarged capture and
reduced-motion capture. Dense inventories were scrolled and searched to their final concrete item
without changing fixture state. Rendered opaque panel/reader text contrast was at least 13:1; no
narrow-view horizontal overflow remained. Transition guards retain visited-source operation counts,
require Enter to add one reader visit, and prevent restore from adding a visit. These checks apply
to this HTML prototype.

All nine cross-journey categories have design representations and evidence. Accessibility coverage
records labels, roles, states, non-color cues and the reading structure; assistive-technology
sessions were not run. The accessibility tree and keyboard delivery are browser evidence, not Xuzz
or assistive-technology validation.

Store discovery, search results, validation, acquisition, playback, lifecycle, source/storage
effects, authoring and commit/retry outcomes remain simulated. The proposed authoring protocol does
not establish production atomicity. Real store diffs, restart/network/cache behavior, cryptography,
playback, sovereign keymap integration, assistive technology and graphics-backend behavior remain
the specification’s post-implementation checks. This completes concept representation, not
implementation or participant usability validation.
