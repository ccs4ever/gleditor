Origin/Back visual audit correction

The initial screen-reader review and my visual inspection labeled the Origin frame as missing its
link panel. That report was mistaken: the preview-based interpretations were inconsistent, whereas decoded
image data agrees. Numeric pixel checks show the docked-panel band is identical for Back and Origin in
the eighth-increment pointer run, the eighth-increment enlarged GL run, and the ninth-increment red
runs. The Origin frame therefore does not show a rendered panel inconsistency in these captures.

For all three Back/Origin pairs, the ROI y=316..551 (800x235) has absolute pixel error AE=0. Each
pair's 8-bit RGB pixel-buffer SHA-256 is identical within the pair. The dominant panel fill is RGB (30,41,59),
with 112,850 pixels in the 800x235 region. `diagnostic-panel/panel-band-back-vs-origin.png`
shows the enlarged ninth-red pair. `diagnostic-panel/pixel-audit.txt` records hashes and metrics.

The original mistaken interpretation is preserved in `findings-original.txt` for provenance; do
not treat it as a product bug. Source dumps remain unchanged before and after navigation, and each
branch retained two visit records.
