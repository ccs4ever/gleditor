## Outcome boundaries

“Concept supported” is used only for a visibly represented subtask whose affordance and result are
inspectable. It does not imply that a person chose it, that the transition executes, or that
stores/networking behave as pictured. For each base step, the primary outcome is conservative;
partial positive observations are retained separately in `represented_observations`. Absent
necessary transitions are Not represented; unresolved policies are State undefined; visible
fixture/count ambiguity is Confusing.

No keyboard bindings, accessibility tree, measured contrast, reduced-motion run, narrow/enlarged
layout, real persistence, network reply, media playback, commit execution, or restart was tested.
These require prepared concept cards first and later implementation validation.
