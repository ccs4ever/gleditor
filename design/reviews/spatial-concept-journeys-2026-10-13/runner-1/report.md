## Assessment

The Store telescope makes topic and followed-author discovery, exact edition labels and separate
trust/integrity statuses legible. Its paired acquisition image distinguishes interrupted 68/96
transfer from validated Ready V12 and separates author following from opening. Pointer advancement,
separate invalid/unavailable variants, and an offline reopen outcome still need visible states. The
interrupted card also conflicts internally: its subtitle says transfer is active while its status
says interrupted.

Omni lens presents native text, cell, OCR, transcript and title-only video matches in one surface.
It labels cached remote items and partial coverage. It does not provide per-result origin-store
identity, exact OCR/transcript locators, equal-text/shared-source comparison, stale-request
handling, or the result lifecycle required by J19. The history image explicitly distinguishes
independent equal-text sources and shows a historical removal, but its matching-rule details and
missing/empty coverage states remain incomplete. Its lower tray also shows three result-like cards
beneath a “2 shown of 3” label, which leaves the historical card’s role unclear. A separate expanded
investigation frame does show integrated C17 V8 as a historical pressure match at 3.14 and pinned
V12 as 3.16 with no pressure match; it does not show the explicit query-switch sequence required by
J20/3.

The Comparison prism clearly represents its two-state/shared-prefix subtask: both Store A / Run B
states are pinned, the original `pressure:` range is distinguished from changed values, and the
footer names an independent fixture. The whole J21/1 step remains Not represented because
independently equal-text passages are absent. This must remain distinct from the integrated fixture
where C17 V12 has no pressure match. The prism is only two-state and text/value oriented; it does
not represent branch ancestry, topology changes, unavailable-region retention, or reopen.

## Cross-check repeats

All nine repeats for each of J17–J21 are recorded in `results.json` (45 records total). Pointer
discoverability can be assessed only for pictured controls. The static desktop images do not
establish list/keyboard equivalence, accessible names/tree, narrow-density behavior, enlarged
optics, reduced motion, storage behavior, or runtime lifecycle. Relationship language is visibly
explicit in the J20 independent-source note and J21 shared-prefix mapping; J17/J18/J19 do not
establish their relevant source relations. No human behavior is inferred.

## Findings

### R1-01 · major

Journeys/steps: J17/3, J17/4, J17/5.

The concept distinguishes request identity, interrupted transfer and ready/offline status, but omits
the publisher pointer change, invalid/unavailable states and reopen outcome. Add separate cards for
V12 pending plus pointer V13, invalid payload, unavailable peers, and cached-offline reopen; retain
request/version identity and show author-follow/layer ledger unchanged or explicitly changed.
Acquisition concepts are partially legible; exact-version lifecycle is not ready for interaction
validation.

### R1-02 · major

Journeys/steps: J18/2, J18/3, J18/5.

Cards distinguish evidence type, version and some held states, but source store is absent; OCR
region and transcript end locator are absent; equal-text versus shared-source inspection and return
result are unpictured. Give every result a readable origin store/version/evidence descriptor; add
OCR coordinates and transcript time range with derived-text/source distinction; include separate
equal-address/equal-text and shared-source occurrence cards plus entered/returned state. Cross-media
matching is visually promising, but provenance and locator explanation need cards before user
testing.

### R1-03 · major

Journeys/steps: J19/2, J19/3, J19/4, J19/5.

Filter/group controls and a partial coverage summary are present; excluded selection, stale
response, detailed unsupported surface, post-cancel retention, empty states, result snapshots and
rerun are absent. Add filter-excluded inspector state with clear/restore, request IDs with
stale-response handling, coverage detail including unsupported OCR, cancel-retained results,
completed-empty versus incomplete-zero cards, and distinct saved recipe/result snapshot/re-run
controls. Query refinement and result lifecycle cannot yet be evaluated as a complete workflow.

### R1-04 · major

Journeys/steps: J20/2, J20/3, J20/4, J20/5.

The image shows text/whole-token/number/related controls and partial history coverage, but no
numeric normalization/unit rule, state-run expansion or empty/missing state distinctions. The
separate expanded investigation frame already shows the integrated C17 V8 historical 3.14 match and
pinned V12 3.16 no-pressure-match state, but not the explicit query-switch transition from 3.14 to
literal pressure with case folding or retention of the historical reference. The “2 shown of 3”
count also conflicts with the three visible result-like cards (R1-06). Add a transition card for
that query/rule change while retaining the existing integrated fixture; show matching-rule detail
for numeric units, expanded occurrence/state/op counts, missing versus completed-empty branch
states, and separate recipe from captured results. Historical query controls are visible, while
their semantics and completeness states remain unvalidated.

### R1-05 · major

Journeys/steps: J21/1, J21/2, J21/3, J21/4, J21/5.

The independent two-state text/value prism is clear; independent equal-text examples, required
three-state ancestry, topology, unavailable target descriptor and reopen/entered outcomes are
absent. Add independent equal-text source examples, a three-state ancestry card grounded in actual
parent identity, reciprocal topology/membership difference cards with neighborhoods,
unavailable-region inspector retaining target identity, and explicit enter/return/save/reopen cards.
The two-state text/value concept is coherent; independently equal-text examples and broader
comparison behavior need designed evidence before testing.
