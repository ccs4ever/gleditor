## Persistence boundary

Opening or closing a view changes `Session::generation()` and redraws the panel but does not replace
the selected Link's occurrence snapshot. The same-session recovery route therefore retains the
chosen exact site. `restoreSelection()` creates a new selection and resolves its saved numeric
cursors against the currently open views; `supply()` only clamps indices. A fresh resolution with a
different occurrence order can therefore change the exact site denoted by the same ordinal. The
saved visit destination and selected endpoint cursor are different evidence. Same-profile restart
identity preservation remains a J25 task; no activity format or compatibility reader changes here.
