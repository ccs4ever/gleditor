# Search and comparison state-pack coverage (J17–J21)

The pack contains five independent fixtures, each with a full initial state and a continuous
five-step action chain. Reading panels retain short source paragraphs through explicit Enter
actions. Source operation counts are fixed during discovery, filtering, inspection, comparison,
entry, restore and save. Only reader activity is incremented by explicit Enter; restore does not add
a visit. Reader collection writes are limited to the explicitly saved recipe, snapshot and
comparison descriptors. No action writes authored destination operations.
