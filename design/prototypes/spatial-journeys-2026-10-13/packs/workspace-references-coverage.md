## Fixture and effect boundaries

- J22/J24 use only the integrated Review V4 / visit 03 workspace, with L17 origin 01 and complete
  2-left/3-right endsets. C17 V8/V12 comparison is not merged with L17 Right 2, which is C24 V8.
- J25 uses an independent five-visit reader activity fixture: 01→02→03 and 01→02→04→05. It is not
  J22/J24’s integrated four-visit path.
- Package identities are immutable carrier digests. Counts use enabled and retained membership
  separately. The explicit selected route switches to #13 while retaining #12 and L17 identity;
  choices that only review or retain #13 are separate variants.
- J26 starts with a deterministic 146-card Pressure review dataset and a separate Check later pocket
  for the five newly saved typed references. The complete items array is exposed through the pouch
  panel’s allRowsPath; literal rows/count.visible remain the current six-item window. Filtering
  operates on the 146-item Pressure review: 18 match, six are visible, and ref:018 remains
  list-reachable off-window. Explicit duplicate memberships then increase the active count from 146
  to 149.
- Inspection, folding, search/filtering, preview, package emphasis and simulated availability events
  do not change visited source operation counts. Explicit Enter adds exactly one activity visit;
  Resume/Back/Forward restore existing nodes. Explicit saves affect reader collection/activity
  metadata only. Forge selection is private staging; no destination operation is authored.

No step is left as a TBD or future-state stub. Variants provide executable alternative/negative
paths for invalid signature, package review/retention alternatives, unavailable links, overlapping
Link identity, unavailable Walk target, duplicate identity, typed availability and forge
eligibility.
