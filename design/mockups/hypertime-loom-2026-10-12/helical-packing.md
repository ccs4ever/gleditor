# Helical packing before aggregation

Status: visual design proposal. This adds an intermediate geometric packing stage to the dense
liquid-glass Hypertime Loom. Counts and identifiers in the generated image are illustrative.

## Packing sequence

A long operation run can be shown as a straight stretch, an open helix, a tighter helix, and finally
a counted skein. Each stage presents the same operation identities and ancestry. Coiling is a layout
change, not a history cycle, a new dimension, or a new stored operation.

Chronological order follows the continuous spine's arclength. Sparse direction marks, entrance and
exit IDs, and turn-range labels help distinguish neighbors along the path from chips that happen to
lie on adjacent turns. A selected trunk is a named continuation path; coiling does not give it extra
authority over another branch.

## Local opening

The focused turn increases its pitch and opens a reading pocket. Nearby compact turns absorb local
slack while the selected operation and pinned anchors stay steady. The transition is reversible.
Readable labels still use stable detail levels with hysteresis; geometric interpolation does not
shrink text into illegibility.

A coil retains a visible hollow core and distinguishable turns. Op chips have hard edges and remain
individually reachable by chronological stepping, direct selection, or local opening. A rear-facing
operation can be brought into its reading context without requiring the reader to orbit blindly. If
tighter winding would bury the ports or focus, use a counted skein instead of adding more occluded
turns.

## Relations and context

Forks leave their exact operation ports, with separate exits and branch counts. A rear fork has an
explicit visible port marker. Adjacent turns acquire no edges merely from their proximity.

The helical history spine is neutral silver. Gold transclusion strands cross through or around the
coil independently and terminate on shared content spans. The colors do not change when the run
coils or opens.

A tugged container-history track can coil too. It remains another view of the same operations, with
correspondence to their source positions. Opened individual operations keep identified origin
markers; compact regions use visibly aggregated correspondence.

Coiling packs individually represented operations into less space. Counted aggregation and a bounded
visible set remain the later scale mechanisms. The mockup does not establish rendering cost or a
maximum store size.
