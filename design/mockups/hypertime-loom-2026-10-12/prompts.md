# Hypertime Loom mockups

Concept images for a suspended liquid-glass hypertime loom and container-thread history focus.
Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. These are design proposals, not captures of the app.

## Images

- `01-suspended-loom.png`: hanging and bowed gold transclusion weave, with silver ancestry and
  container filaments.
- `02-container-thread-tug.png`: tugging Cell C17's container thread reveals a second spatial
  appearance of its operations.
- `03-parallel-branch-history.png`: reachable branch history in a foreground track, tethered back to
  original operations.

The intended relationship mapping is gold for shared primedia, silver for ancestry and containment,
and violet for explicit links. The contextual track is another view of existing operation
identities; revealing it does not move the source topology or author new operations. The labelled
operation IDs and branch topology in these generated concepts are illustrative and require
deterministic layout in a future implementation.

## Generation prompts

See [refinement prompts](refinement-prompts.md) for the targeted edits used for the final second and
third images.
