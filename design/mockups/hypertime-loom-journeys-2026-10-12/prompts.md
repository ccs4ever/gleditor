# Hypertime Loom journey image prompts

Mode: built-in `image_gen`; use case: `ui-mockup`. No CLI fallback and no application code.

Six final storyboards accompany `journeys.md`: `01-save-and-reopen-op.png`, `02-span-effects.png`,
`03-content-search-v2.png`, `04-cell-topology.png`, `05-page-footprint.png`, and
`06-branch-recovery.png`.

Original generations used `../hypertime-loom-2026-10-12/10-scenario-3-ten-branches-v2.png` as a
style reference. Refinements edited their preceding generated storyboard. The prompts below are
exact, including the targeted correction prompts.
