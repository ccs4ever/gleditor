Commentary on Xanadulogical Systems and Multi-Rank Manifolds

Section A: On the Interconnected Fabric of Knowledge
The fundamental axiom of Xanadu is that EVERYTHING IS DEEPLY INTERTWINGLED. In an important sense there are no 'subjects' at all; there is only all knowledge, since the brute facts, but the aspects of reality and the thoughts which have already been thought are interconnectable into the same great tangle.

Section B: Dialectic Critique of Web Hierarchies
Conventional hierarchical filesystems and one-way web links force artificial hierarchies onto fluid knowledge networks. Xanadu replaces static URLs with content-addressed primedia coordinates and sovereign permascrolls.

Section C: The Eightfold Relational Taxonomy
Links connect ideas through eight distinct semantic modes: commentary, illustration, disagreement, authorship, quotation, associative context, formatting presentation, and multi-dimensional zigzag manifolds.
