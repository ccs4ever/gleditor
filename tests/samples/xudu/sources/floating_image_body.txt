Figure 1.2: A small figure floats at the column's left edge so the
paragraph beside it keeps flowing rather than stepping over it the way
a full-width figure would. Xanadu's own visible-connection principle
extends naturally to layout: a reader should never lose their place in
a line of argument just because a diagram happened to land nearby. The
docuverse is not a stack of isolated pages -- text and figure share the
same band of the page, each legible without eclipsing the other. Once
the paragraph runs far enough down the page to clear the figure's own
height, the column widens back out to its full measure and later lines
sit exactly where they would have if no figure had ever floated beside
them at all.
